Spraywall Twister
-----------------

Step 1: Get a few climbing buddies

Step 2: Press "Roll" button

Step 3: Yell out which limb to use on which color hold

Step 4: Watch them sweat

Bonus 1: The 🧠 icon enables the player on the wall to use their favorite limb.

Bonus 2: Sometimes, the player will be allowed to choose a hold of their own choosing. Choose wisely.


App is currently deployed at

https://romunov.shinyapps.io/spraytwister
